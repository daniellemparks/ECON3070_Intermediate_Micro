\section*{Problem Set 5}

\emph{Each student must submit your own \textbf{HANDWRITTEN} work. Turn in a paper copy at the beginning of lecture on 11/2/2026.  Please write your name on each page and make sure your problem set is stapled.  Students are allowed to work in groups, but each student must turn in their own set of answers.  No help from outside of the course is allowed.  To receive full credit for a problem, all work must be shown.  Partial credit will be awarded.  All problems are weighted evenly.}


\begin{enumerate}
  \item Suppose that a firm's short-run total cost function is given by $STC(Q) = Q^2 + 200Q + 100$ 
  \begin{enumerate}
  	\item Write the firm's short-run profit maximization problem
     \vspace*{25mm}  	
    \item Solve for the firm's short-run supply function.
     \vspace*{60mm}
     \item For what range of prices will the firm choose not to produce any quantity of output?
     \vspace*{20mm}       
     \end{enumerate}
    Now suppose that this is the only firm in the market and that market demand is given by $Q_D = 950 - 2P$
    \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(d)] Solve for the short-run market equilibrium quantity and price.
  \end{enumerate}
  
  \newpage
  \item Suppose that each firm in a perfectly competitive market has long-run cost represented as $TC(Q) = 100Q^2 - 100Q + 100$.
  \begin{enumerate}
	\item What are the three conditions for a long-run perfectly competitive equilibrium?    
  \vspace*{40mm} 
    \item If the firm's goal is to maximize profit in a perfectly competitive market, find the quantity that each firm will choose to sell.
\vspace*{65mm}
    \item At what price will firms sell each unit?
      \vspace*{45mm} 
    \item Suppose that the market demand is given by $Q_D = 2000 - 5P$. How many firms will enter the market in the long run?
  \end{enumerate}
  \newpage
  Now suppose that, due to changing tastes, consumer demand has shifted outward, such that $Q_D = 2400 - 6P$
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(e)] Find the new price and quantity that each firm will sell the good at in the long-run (assuming constant input prices).
\vspace*{75mm}
    \item[(f)] How many additional firms will enter the market, in response to the increased demand?
  \end{enumerate}
  \newpage
  \item Suppose that domestic demand is given by $Q_D = 5000 - 100P$ and the domestic supply curve is given by $Q_S = 150P$.  The world price for this good is $P = 10$.  Suppose that a 50\% tariff on the price of this good is being considered.
  \begin{enumerate}
	\item Draw a graph illustrating the free trade equilibrium (with no tariff).  Label all relevant parts of the graph including equilibrium price and quantity \& producer and consumer surplus.   
  \vspace*{60mm} 
    \item By how much would the tariff increase producer surplus for domestic suppliers?
\vspace*{40mm}
    \item How much would the government collect in tariff revenues?
      \vspace*{44mm} 
    \item What is the dead-weight loss from the tariff?
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
